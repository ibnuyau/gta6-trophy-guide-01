    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone — which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. Around 200 hours at a 4-out-of-10 difficulty, one playthrough, no online trophies.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is flagged missable by the published guides. Every story trophy is unmissable, and everything outside the story stays available after the credits — including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist. The game has no mission replay, so the safety net is manual saves: one at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission.

        Why the rest are safe

        Story trophies (12) pop as you play. Side finales, robberies, collectibles, activities and the 100% checklist all remain open after the story, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion. Cheats switch trophies and saving off, so leave them off on your trophy save.

        About & sources

        Leonida Ledger is an offline guide and tracker for the 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned, none of them online. It works with the network off and saves nothing anywhere but your own browser.

        Where the facts come from

        Every trophy's name, description and grade are the official text as PlayStation publishes it, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles — the two agreed on every name, description and grade. Each guide is written for this entry in our own words and names its sources beside it. Nothing is copied from another guide: a source is cited, never copied.

        Honesty note

        The game released on 19 November 2026. These guides are built from the official trophy descriptions and the published guides above — they have not been checked in the game by us. Where a detail is unconfirmed (for example the exact trigger of a hidden trophy with a vague description), the guide says so rather than inventing a step. No Rockstar Games logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this guide; the look is our own.

        Your progress stays yours

        Progress is saved in this browser's localStorage only. Export downloads a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser, and Import reads it back. Nothing is sent anywhere.
